A Trusted Place for Recovery in Jhapa

Jiban Kalyan Samaj Sudhar Kendra is a drug and alcohol rehabilitation center in Kakarvitta, Jhapa. We support people in recovery through education, yoga, meditation and spiritual growth, with care for the whole person and their family.

  • Education, yoga and meditation based approach
  • Located in Mechinagar, Kakarvitta, Jhapa
  • Open to patients from all over Nepal
